Cookie policy

Last updated 20 November 2026

No cookies, two remembered settings

Groove Radar does not set any cookies and does not use tracking pixels or device fingerprinting. It keeps two small entries in your browser's local storage, which stay on your device and are never sent to this site or anyone else:

  • gr-near: the place you picked under "Measure from", so the page measures "near you" from it next time. It is only saved when you pick a city or the default, and removed when you switch back to "My location". It stays until you change it or clear your browser data.
  • gr-intro: the date the opening animation last played, so it only plays on your first visit each day. It stays until you clear your browser data.

Both only make the site do what you would expect it to remember, so no consent banner is needed under the UK Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations, the German TDDDG and the EU ePrivacy rules. To remove them, clear the site data for drfeelpoo.uk in your browser settings.

Third parties

There is no analytics, advertising, social media or other third-party code on the site, and the fonts are served from this site itself. The site is delivered by Cloudflare, which does not set cookies for this site under its current settings. The "near you" feature uses your approximate location from your connection without storing the location on your device; see the privacy policy. If you click a link to another website, such as a ticket seller or a music shop, that website may use its own cookies under its own policy.

If this changes

If the site ever needs cookies or anything else stored on your device, this page will list each one with its purpose and how long it lasts, and anything that is not strictly necessary will only run after you have agreed to it.
