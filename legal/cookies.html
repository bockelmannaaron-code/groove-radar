Cookie policy

Last updated 20 November 2026

This site uses no cookies

Groove Radar does not set any cookies, and it does not use similar technologies such as local storage, session storage, tracking pixels or device fingerprinting. Nothing is saved on your device when you use it.

That is also why there is no cookie banner: under the UK Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations, the German TDDDG and the EU ePrivacy rules, consent is only needed when a site stores or reads information on your device, and this one does not.

Third parties

There is no analytics, advertising, social media or other third-party code on the site, and the fonts are served from this site itself. The site is delivered by Cloudflare, which does not set cookies for this site under its current settings. The "near you" feature uses your approximate location from your connection without storing anything on your device; see the privacy policy. If you click a link to another website, such as a ticket seller or a music shop, that website may use its own cookies under its own policy.

If this changes

If the site ever needs cookies or similar technologies, this page will list each one with its purpose and how long it lasts, and anything that is not strictly necessary will only run after you have agreed to it.
