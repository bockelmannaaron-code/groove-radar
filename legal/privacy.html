The short version

What happens when you visit

When your browser asks for a page, it sends technical information that every web request contains: your IP address, the address of the page, the time, and your browser and device type. This site is delivered by Cloudflare, Inc., 101 Townsend Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA, which processes this information to deliver the page and to protect the site against attacks and abuse.

We do not collect, store or look at visitor logs ourselves, and request logging in our hosting settings is switched off. Cloudflare may keep security and operational logs for a short time under its own privacy policy.

The legal basis is our legitimate interest in running a website that works and stays secure (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR and UK GDPR). Cloudflare is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework and its UK extension, and also uses the European Commission's standard contractual clauses for transfers to the USA.

Your approximate location

When the page loads, it asks this site for your approximate location so it can mark gigs within 150 km of you as "near you". Cloudflare, our hosting provider, already estimates this from your IP address for every request; the site passes back only the city, country and rounded coordinates (about 1 km precision) to your browser. The distances are worked out in your browser. Your location is not saved by us, not sent anywhere else, not linked to you, and not logged.

The page shows which place it used ("Near you means within 150 km of …") and you can change it to another city, or to the default (the Munich area and the Rheingau). If you pick a city or the default, that choice is remembered in your own browser (never sent to us) so the next visit uses it too; switching back to "My location" removes it. Your IP-based location can be wrong, for example when you use a VPN.

The legal basis is our legitimate interest in showing you relevant gigs (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR and UK GDPR). Because the detected location is only estimated for the request and never stored, no cookie or consent banner is involved. If you object, pick a city or the default in the page; it will not use your detected location again until you switch back.

Ask Groove Radar (AI assistant)

The "Ask Groove Radar" button opens an assistant that answers questions about the listed artists, events and trips. It only runs when you send a question. Each time you do, this site passes the following to Anthropic, PBC (San Francisco, USA), whose AI model Claude writes the answer: the conversation so far in that window, the gig list shown on this site, today's date, and the place the page measures "near you" from (a city and rounded coordinates). Your IP address is not passed on. To answer, the assistant may search the web; those searches are run by Anthropic, not from your device.

We do not store your questions or the answers, and the conversation is gone when you close or reload the page. Anthropic processes them to produce the answer and keeps them for a limited time under its own privacy policy; under its commercial terms it does not use them to train its models. Don't type personal details you don't want to share.

The legal basis is that you ask for the answer (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR and UK GDPR), and our legitimate interest in offering the assistant (Article 6(1)(f)). Data sent to Anthropic in the USA is protected by the European Commission's standard contractual clauses. To limit misuse, the site counts how many questions come from one connection per minute; that count is not stored.

Gig and music information

The site lists public performances by DJs and producers, using their stage names, together with venues, dates and links to ticket and record shops. This comes from publicly available listings such as Songkick, venue and ticket websites, and Beatport. If you are one of the artists listed and want something corrected or removed, email us and we will deal with it promptly.

Links to other websites

Ticket, event and music links take you to other websites (for example ticket sellers, Songkick, Beatport, Bandcamp, Traxsource and Juno). Nothing is sent to them until you click a link. Once you are on their site, their own privacy policies apply.
