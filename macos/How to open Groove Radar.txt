HOW TO OPEN GROOVE RADAR

1. Drag Groove Radar onto the Applications folder in this window.

2. Open Groove Radar from Applications (or Launchpad).

The first time, your Mac shows "Groove Radar Not Opened" and says
Apple could not verify it. That's normal for apps from independent
developers that aren't sold through the App Store. Groove Radar
contains no malware. To open it:

On macOS 15 Sequoia or later:
  a. Click "Done" on the message.
  b. Open System Settings > Privacy & Security.
  c. Scroll down to "Groove Radar was blocked..." and click "Open Anyway".
  d. Enter your Mac password, then click "Open Anyway" once more.

On macOS 13 Ventura or 14 Sonoma:
  a. In Applications, right-click (or Control-click) Groove Radar.
  b. Choose "Open", then click "Open" in the message.

You only do this once. After that, Groove Radar opens normally.

Groove Radar needs macOS 13 or later and works on Apple silicon and Intel Macs.
